EF 3D Studio

A browser viewer for 3D models and reconstruction datasets, powered by Spirula Studio by harry7557558 and contributors. EF Ventures supplies this themed adaptation; the original authors are not affiliated with or endorsing EF Ventures.

Your files

Files selected or dropped into the viewer are processed in your browser. This pilot has no uploads, cloud storage, shared member library, or analytics. Closing or refreshing the page clears the loaded session. Opening a model URL fetches that model and its referenced companion files from its host.

Using the viewer

  • Choose files or drop a model into the viewport. Keep OBJ/GLTF textures and companion files together.
  • Try Explore demo for the included EF Orbit mesh. Drag to rotate and scroll or pinch to zoom. Use the settings menu on smaller screens.
  • This page views existing models. Photo/video reconstruction and training are features of the separate native Spirula Studio application.
